Soul Be It

Open the presentation in Canva

Three gods. Three mythologies. One team standing in their way.

Challenge three mythical bosses — the Phoenix, Loki, and Poseidon — in a multiplayer action game centred around challenging, reactive boss encounters.

Building upon my previous Souls-like boss project, this project greatly expands the complexity of the boss AI, introducing multi-phase encounters, deeper attack patterns, and bosses capable of reacting dynamically to player behaviour.

The game also features three playable classes with unique movesets and abilities, supported by globally hosted multiplayer infrastructure that allows players to create and join independent game sessions.

Features

  • Reactive Combo Decision-Making
  • Hierarchical Boss AI Architecture
  • Scalable Multi-Phase Boss System
  • Dedicated Multiplayer Server Architecture
  • Networked Gameplay & Voice Communication
  • Multi-Class Player Architecture
