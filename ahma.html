Ahma's Stray search

A quiet, cozy game about looking out for the small ones left behind.

After watching a news report about a rise in abandoned cats, Ahma decides to do what she can—by feeding the strays in her neighbourhood and keeping track of them in hopes of helping them find homes.

Explore spaces like void decks and gardens, spot hidden cats, and gently interact with them through feeding and petting. Along the way, watch the story unfold through 5 cutscenes that show how simple acts of care can influence those around us.

Features

  • A calm 3D exploration and cat-collecting experience
  • Feed, pet, and record cats in your album
  • 5 story cutscenes that reveal Ahma’s growing connection with her community
