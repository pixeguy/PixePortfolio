Surface Tension

A heart-warming drag-and-drop narrative game.

You play as Adam, a boy who has to “mask” his intense emotions that are constantly misunderstood.

Navigate daily conversations by dragging and dropping “mask” fragments onto his face to cover his true feelings before time runs out.

Can you help Adam have a peaceful day?

Features

  • Drag and Drop
  • State-Driven Dialogue System
