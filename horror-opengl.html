Horror Game in 3D OpenGL

Reflections on a 3D Carnival Horror Game.

This project challenged me to combine technical rendering knowledge with atmosphere-driven game design. Building a 3D carnival horror game in OpenGL made lighting, physics, and visual consistency especially important in creating tension and immersion.

By carefully tuning lighting, shadows, and interactions, I learned how technical decisions directly affect mood and player experience. The project pushed me to think holistically, ensuring that rendering, physics, and gameplay systems all worked together seamlessly.

Scope

  • Managing rendering and gameplay systems in a OpenGL setup
  • Using lighting to establish horror atmosphere and mood
