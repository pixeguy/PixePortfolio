Souls Like

Reflections on Souls-Like Combat and Boss Design in 3D Unity.

This project focused on recreating the deliberate and challenging feel of Souls-like combat within a 3D Unity environment. Designing the boss encounter required careful tuning of player movement, attack timing, and enemy behavior to create a fair but demanding experience.

Through iteration and playtesting, I learned how strongly combat balance and feedback influence player perception. Small adjustments to animations, hitboxes, and telegraphs significantly changed how readable and satisfying the fight felt.

Scope

  • Designing timing-based combat that rewards precision and patience
  • Structuring boss AI using states, phases, and attack patterns
  • Using animation, hitboxes, and telegraphs to communicate danger clearly
