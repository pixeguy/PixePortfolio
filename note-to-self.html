Note To Self

You saw it. You wrote it down. So it must be true…right?

Escape a cursed realm by navigating mazes filled with mysterious effect pads. During the Vision Phase, uncover their effects and leave notes for your future self.

When the maze begins, those notes are all you have to guide you — but can you really trust what you wrote?

Features

  • Data-Driven Effect System
  • Unity Tilemap
  • Modular Trigger Architecture
