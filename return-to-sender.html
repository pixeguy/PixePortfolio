Re:turn to Sender

Special delivery! Take control of the mail and launch your way to the mailman.

Play as a piece of mail determined to deliver itself! Platform through obstacles and journey backwards through different eras of mail delivery, from the modern day to the earliest forms of postal service.

Reach the mailman, avoid becoming lost mail, and make sure your delivery arrives on time.

Features

  • Physics-Based Launching
  • Physics Trajectory Prediction
  • Modular Difficulty System
